---
term: Integer
definition: An integer is just a whole number, like 1, 2 or 3. It has no bits after a dot, so 4 is an integer, but 4.5 isn't.
category: Data & Variables
example: Counting down 3… 2… 1… uses integers, but a price like £4.99 isn't an integer because it has numbers after the dot.
